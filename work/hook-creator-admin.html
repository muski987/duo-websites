Case study · 03

Hook Creator Admin

A private dashboard where Hook adds creators and brands to hookmgmt.in themselves, with Instagram stats that keep themselves up to date.

Client
Hook, talent management for AI & tech creators
Role
Product design & full-stack development
Year
2026
Access
Internal tool — demo on request
Typical build time
15–20 days for a tool with a backend

Internal tool · Admin dashboard · Instagram API · Next.js · Supabase · Netlify

The problem

The creator roster on hookmgmt.in was written into the website's code. Every new creator, new photo or change of niche needed a developer, and the follower and view counts went stale within weeks.

Hook signs creators quickly, so the site was often behind the real roster when brands were looking at it.

The solution

A password-protected dashboard where the team adds a creator with a photo, name, Instagram handle and niches, and the creator shows up on hookmgmt.in within seconds.

Followers, average views and engagement are pulled from Instagram automatically every 4 hours, so nobody has to type numbers in again.

Screenshots are from a local copy of the dashboard. Creator names and photos are the ones already public on hookmgmt.in.

Key features

  • Add, edit, reorder and remove creators without touching code
  • Changes go live on hookmgmt.in within seconds
  • Followers, average views and engagement sync from Instagram every 4 hours
  • Phone photos are shrunk automatically before upload, so the site stays fast
  • Brands tab for the "Brands we've worked with" strip
  • Every save keeps a backup of the previous list, so mistakes can be undone
  • Works on mobile

How it's built

  • Dashboard: Next.js, hosted on Netlify, separate from the public website
  • Storage: Supabase holds the creator list, brand list and photos, which the website reads
  • Live updates: each save tells the website to refresh its pages straight away
  • Stats: the Instagram Graph API, synced on a schedule every 4 hours
  • Security: the password and keys stay on the server, and the login cookie is signed and can't be read by scripts

Outcome

Hook's team can now update its own roster the day a creator signs, and the numbers brands see on the site stay current.
