Titan Studios

A site for a film production studio, themed on its own black-and-white logo.

The brief

Titan Studios is a production house for brands, musicians and filmmakers: feature films, commercials, music videos, documentaries and series, handled by one crew from script to final export.

The site had to feel like their films and lead visitors to start a project.

The direction

The direction comes straight from the studio's logo: black and white, with one red accent used sparingly so it reads like a title card rather than a template.

Large type, a "Scroll to enter" opening and slow, scroll-led motion, so moving through the site feels like a title sequence.

What we built

  • An opening "Scroll to enter" reveal with a showreel link
  • Work by category: feature films, music videos, commercials and documentaries
  • Services across pre-production, production and post-production
  • A four-step process, from brief to shoot and delivery
  • An enquiry form with project type, plus email, WhatsApp and Instagram
