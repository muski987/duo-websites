GrowMedex

A product catalogue and export site for Grow MedEx Pharmaceuticals, a pharmaceutical sourcing and export company from Amravati, Maharashtra, shipping worldwide.

Industry
Pharmaceutical export
Year
2026
Status
Live
Site

Typical build time
15–20 days for an online store

The brief

Grow MedEx Pharmaceuticals is based in Amravati, Maharashtra, and sources and ships medicines to business buyers internationally as a verified exporter.

The site had to make a catalogue of 600+ products easy to browse, and make it quick for a buyer to get in touch.

The direction

Clean white pages in the green and navy of the GrowMedex logo, with photography of real production lines, so the site reads as a serious, trusted supplier rather than a shop.

What we built

  • Home, About, Products, Best Sellers and Contact pages
  • A catalogue of 600+ products, sorted by dosage form (tablets, capsules, drops, injectables and more) and by therapy
  • A best sellers section for the most ordered products
  • WhatsApp and Telegram buttons, phone and email, and 24×7 support details on every page
