City Rental

A rental site built around the red, white and shield shape of the brand's badge.

Industry
Bike & car rental
Year
2026
Status
Demo approved · in development
Site
View the demo (opens in a new tab)
Typical build time
10–12 days for the demo

The brief

City Rental rents cars, bikes and scooters by the day, with every vehicle serviced before each trip.

The site had to show the fleet and let a customer check availability in a few taps.

The direction

Everything on the site is taken from the City Rental badge: its red, its white, and its shield shape, which repeats through the layout.

Bold red headlines and a car photo cut into the shield shape lead the page, with the booking bar right under it.

What we built

  • A booking bar: vehicle type, pickup area and dates, then check availability
  • The fleet in four groups (sedans, coupes, bikes and scooters) with daily prices
  • A featured ride with seats, gearbox, fuel and price
  • A weekend package and the pre-trip service checklist
  • How it works in three steps, plus WhatsApp and call buttons
