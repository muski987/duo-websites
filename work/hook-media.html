Hook Media Management

A creator roster for Hook Media Management, a talent agency that connects tech brands with tech creators.

Industry
Creator & influencer management
Year
2026
Status
Live
Site
Visit hookmgmt.in (opens in a new tab)
Typical build time
10–12 days for a portfolio site

The brief

Hook manages tech creators in India and runs campaigns for SaaS and tech brands. Their line is "Let's get the masses hooked."

The site had to show the whole roster at a glance and turn a brand's visit into a campaign enquiry.

The direction

Dark and quiet, so the creators' photos carry the page. Each profile puts niche and reach up front, the way a brand compares creators before a campaign.

What we built

  • A roster of creator profiles with photo, niche tags, total reach and social links
  • Filters by niche, such as Tech, AI and Ed-tech
  • A campaign shortlist: brands bookmark creators to build their own roster
  • "Start a campaign" and "Partner with Hook" enquiries, plus team contacts

Outcome

Hook now has all of its work and its whole creator roster in one place. On calls with brands, the team shares the site on screen to walk through the roster and explain each creator.
