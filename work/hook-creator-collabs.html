Case study · 02

Hook Creator Collabs

An internal CRM that makes sure every creator at a talent agency gets a fair share of brand deals.

Client
Hook, talent management for AI & tech creators
Role
Product design & full-stack development
Year
2026
Access
Internal tool — demo on request
Typical build time
15–20 days for a tool with a backend

Internal tool · CRM · Dashboard · Supabase · Vercel

The problem

Hook manages a roster of AI and tech creators and assigns them brand collaborations. Without a shared view, some creators were getting most of the deals while others went weeks without one, and nobody noticed until creators asked.

Payments, deadlines and embargoes were scattered across chats and sheets.

The solution

A secure web dashboard where the team logs every brand collab and instantly sees who's falling behind.

Creators who drop below the team average, or go too long without a collab, are automatically red-flagged at the top, so deals get shared fairly.

All screenshots use sample data, not real creator data.

Key features

  • Automatic red flags: compares each creator to the team, tier or niche average; rules are adjustable
  • Collab tracking: brand, deliverable, value, status, content due dates and embargo times
  • Payment tracking: Pending, Invoiced or Paid, with expected and paid dates
  • Campaign results: views, likes, comments, clicks and sign-ups per collab
  • "Who should get this deal?": ranks creators by how far behind they are
  • Monthly report: one click to print or save as PDF, export CSV, or copy a summary for the founders
  • Manager assignment, with a "My creators" view
  • Creator login: each creator sees only their own collabs, payments and deadlines, read-only
  • Works on mobile

How it's built

  • Frontend: lightweight HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework), deployed on Vercel
  • Backend: Supabase, with a Postgres database and passwordless email login
  • Security: access is enforced in the database with row-level security. Team members see everything, creators can only fetch their own profile, and logged-out visitors get nothing

Outcome

Gave the Hook team one shared place to assign and track brand deals, spot creators who are being left out, and answer creators' "when do I get paid?" questions without digging through chats.
