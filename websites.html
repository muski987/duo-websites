What we build

  • Showcase sites For studios, agencies and service businesses: your work, your team and a clear way to get in touch.
  • Catalogues Products or listings people can browse and filter, like the 600+ product catalogue for GrowMedex.
  • Booking and enquiries Enquiry and booking flows, with Razorpay payments and a simple admin panel where you need them.
  • Launch basics Domain, HTTPS and search basics at launch, tested on phones and laptops.

How it works

  1. Free demo A demo built from your brand and references, for you to approve.
  2. Approval You approve the demo, and we sign a contract with the price, timeline and deliverables.
  3. Build Typically 10–12 days, or 15–20 with a backend or database. We share a live link at each stage.
  4. Launch and care Your domain, hosting and files stay in your accounts, and bug fixes are free for 8–9 months after launch.
