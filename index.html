03 · Process

How a project runs

  1. 01

    Brief

    A call to understand your business, your customers and what the site has to do.

  2. 02

    Direction

    A short design direction built from your brand. You approve it before we build.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build in stages and share a live link at each one, so you see real progress.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Testing on phones and laptops, then your domain, HTTPS and search basics.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Your accounts, your files, and notes on how to update the site.
