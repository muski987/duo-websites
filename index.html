03 · Process

How a project runs

Typically 10–12 days for portfolio and showcase sites, and 15–20 days for sites with a backend or database, like online stores.

  1. 01

    Brief

    A call to understand your business and go through everything: what the site has to do, budget and timeline.

  2. 02

    Demo

    A demo built from your brand and references, for you to approve.

  3. 03

    Contract

    Once you approve the demo, we sign a contract with the price, payment schedule, timeline and deliverables agreed on the call. Work starts only after that.

  4. 04

    Build

    We build in stages and share a live link at each one, so you see real progress.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Testing on phones and laptops, then your domain, HTTPS and search basics.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Your accounts, your files, and notes on how to update the site.

05 · FAQ

Questions clients ask

Do you only work with agencies?

No. We build websites, CRMs and internal tools for any business; agencies are one of the industries we know well.

How long does a project take?

Typically 10–12 days for a portfolio or roster site, and 15–20 days for anything with a backend or database, like a dashboard or a large catalogue. We confirm the timeline for your project after the first call.

How much does it cost?

There are no fixed packages. We quote each project after the first call, once we know what the site or tool has to do.

Do we see something before we commit?

Yes. We make a demo for every site first, and the contract is signed only after you approve it.

How do payments work?

30% when you approve the demo, another 30% once half the work is done, and the remaining 40% before handover.

How many rounds of changes do we get?

Up to 5–6 rounds. Because you approve a demo before we sign, it rarely comes to that, and if something still isn't right, we'll work it out with you.

What do you need from us?

The text you want on the site, your photos and the data the site will show, plus reference sites for the look and feel you like. If you're not sure what to write, we draft the text and suggest ideas at no extra cost. Building spreadsheets or organising your data for you is charged separately.

Will we see progress while you build?

Yes. We share a live link at each stage, so you see real progress, not just a final reveal.

Who owns the site, the hosting and the domain?

You do. Hosting and domain sit in your own accounts, never ours, and at handover you get your files and notes on how to update the site.

Can you work from the spreadsheets we already use?

Yes. We move the sheets your team runs on into a proper tool without stopping day-to-day work. That's how we built the CRM Hook uses to share brand deals fairly.

Can our team and creators log in?

Yes. We build passwordless logins where each person sees only their own data, like the creator view in Hook Creator Collabs.

What do you build with?

Mostly Vercel and Supabase. If you'd rather use another paid platform, we're happy to work with it.

What happens after launch?

Bug fixes are free for the first 8–9 months after launch. Updates and new features after that are quoted on request.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, if you need one. Every site we build goes in our portfolio and we ask each client for a short testimonial; if you need the project kept private for a while or have other conditions, tell us and we'll work something out.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We're based in Mumbai and work with clients in India and abroad, over calls, WhatsApp and email.
