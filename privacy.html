Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DUO (duowebsites.in) is a web design and development studio run by Muskan Motwani and Sai Dhanwate in Mumbai, India. This page explains what personal data we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Things you send us. If you email us, fill in our project form or message us, we keep your name, contact details and what you wrote so we can reply.
  • Public business contact details. To reach businesses that may need a website, we use business names, websites and work email addresses that are publicly listed.
  • Public Threads posts. Our internal tool uses the official Threads API to find public posts where people ask for help with a website. We store only the post id, the post text and the author's username, so we don't reply to the same post twice.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, offer our services, and keep track of who we've already contacted. We never sell, rent or share personal data with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it's kept

In our email inbox and in a private, password-protected tool that only our team can open. Our website itself does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

If you don't want to hear from us, reply "stop" to any message and we won't contact you again. To see or delete the data we hold about you, email admin@duowebsites.in and we'll do it within 30 days.
