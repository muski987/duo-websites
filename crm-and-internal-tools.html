Who it's for

Any business where leads, clients or day-to-day work are tracked in sheets, notebooks and chat groups. For example:

  • Clinics Appointments, patient follow-ups and payments in one place.
  • Coaching institutes Enquiries, batches, fees and attendance.
  • Real estate Leads, site visits and where each deal stands.
  • D2C brands Orders, returns, stock and customer follow-ups.
  • Studios Projects, shoots, client approvals and invoices.
  • Agencies Rosters, brand deals and payouts, like the tools we built for Hook.

What you get

  • Custom CRM Built around how your team actually sells and follows up, not a template you have to bend to.
  • Dashboards The numbers your team asks for every day, on one screen.
  • Admin panels Your team updates the website, products or listings without waiting for a developer.
  • Automation Reminders, red flags and reports that run on their own.

How it works

  1. Free demo We go through how your team works today and show you a working demo of the tool.
  2. Approval You approve the demo, and we sign a contract with the price, timeline and deliverables.
  3. Build in 15–20 days We share a live link at each stage, so you see real progress.
  4. Launch and care Your team gets logins and a walkthrough, and bug fixes are free for 8–9 months after launch.

Case study · Hook Creator Collabs

Hook, a talent agency for AI and tech creators, tracked brand deals across chats and sheets. Some creators were getting most of the deals while others went weeks without one, and nobody noticed until creators asked.

We built a CRM where the team logs every collab, sees payments and deadlines, and gets an automatic red flag when a creator falls behind. Creators log in to see only their own deals and payments.
